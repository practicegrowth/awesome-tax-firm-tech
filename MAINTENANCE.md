# Maintenance standard

## Monthly review

Each month, maintainers review:

- proposed additions and corrections;
- reported broken links;
- obvious vendor name, URL, and product-status changes;
- required disclosures; and
- category clarity and duplication.

## Quarterly editorial review

Each quarter, maintainers recheck a representative sample of entries against vendor primary sources and review whether the taxonomy still reflects current tax-firm workflows.

## Editorial integrity

Entries are selected on relevance and verifiability. Paid placement, undisclosed affiliate treatment, fabricated endorsements, and manufactured engagement are prohibited.

When PracticeGrowth.Tech has a material relationship with a listed vendor, the relationship will be disclosed in the entry.

## Change log

Substantive changes are recorded in Git history and release notes when appropriate.
