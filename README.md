# Awesome Tax Firm Tech

> A curated reference for technology used by modern tax and CPA firms.

PracticeGrowth.Tech maintains this list to help tax practices evaluate the tools that support client service, workflow, document collection, tax preparation, security, and growth. It is designed to be useful to firm owners and the vendors that serve them.

## Important notes

- This is an independent, editorially maintained reference — not a ranking, certification, or endorsement.
- Listings may include commercial products. A listing does not imply a partnership, referral relationship, or compensation unless that relationship is disclosed beside the entry.
- Information can change. Confirm pricing, features, security controls, integrations, and suitability directly with the vendor.
- Nothing here is tax, legal, accounting, compliance, or security advice.

## Inclusion criteria

We include tools that are materially relevant to US tax and CPA-firm operations and that have a clear, publicly verifiable product presence. Entries should be specific, concise, and linked to the vendor's official website or primary documentation.

We do not add tools solely because they are submitted, affiliate programs, generic consumer apps without a tax-firm use case, or products with claims we cannot verify.
