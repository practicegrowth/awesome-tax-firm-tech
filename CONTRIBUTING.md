# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this reference.

## Before submitting

1. Confirm that the product has a clear use case for US tax or CPA-firm operations.
2. Link to the vendor's official website or primary documentation.
3. Check that the tool is not already listed.
4. Keep the description factual, brief, and free of promotional claims.

## Evidence and claim boundaries

Use the product's current official page or primary documentation as the source for a proposed description. Do not infer or claim any of the following unless the cited source directly supports it:

- pricing, free tiers, or contract terms;
- integrations, implementation effort, or workflow outcomes;
- security controls, certifications, privacy posture, or regulatory compliance;
- tax, legal, accounting, or policy suitability; or
- comparative, ranking, or performance claims.

When an exact claim cannot be verified, use a narrower description of the product's stated category or purpose. If a vendor has renamed, retired, or materially changed a product, include the relevant primary-source link in the correction.

## Submissions

Open an issue or pull request with:

- product name;
- official URL;
- proposed category;
- a one-sentence factual description; and
- the primary-source URL supporting the description; and
- any relevant disclosure, including an employment, partner, affiliate, or referral relationship.

We may edit, decline, move, or remove entries to preserve scope and quality. Inclusion is editorially independent and is never guaranteed by payment, partnership, or referral status.

## Corrections and removals

If an entry is inaccurate, outdated, or no longer available, please open an issue with the relevant primary source. We aim to review corrections promptly.
